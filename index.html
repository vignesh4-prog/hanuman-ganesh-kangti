01Who we are

A festival built
around people.

For more than 20 years, Hanuman Ganesh Youth Association - Kangti has brought the spirit of Ganesha into everyday life through worship, service, art and the simple joy of showing up for one another.

This year, we celebrate more than 20 years of community, devotion and togetherness. What began with a few young devotees has grown into a welcoming community that serves with care and keeps our traditions alive for the next generation.

09The story of Ganesha

From clay
to blessing.

Ganesha is welcomed as the remover of obstacles, the guardian of new beginnings and a reminder that wisdom grows when it is shared.

Long ago, Parvati shaped a child from the sacred earth and sandalwood paste of her devotion. She gave him life, placed him at the entrance and asked him to guard her peaceful home. When Shiva returned and the young guardian stood faithfully in his way, a difficult misunderstanding changed the child forever.

Parvati's love could not be broken. Shiva recognised the courage and innocence of the boy, and Ganesha received an elephant's head, a sign of memory, patience and deep understanding. He became the beloved son of Shiva and Parvati, and the gentle remover of obstacles for everyone who calls on him with a sincere heart.

That is why the festival begins with welcome. Families clean their homes, bring Ganesha close, offer flowers and modaks, light lamps and sing together. For ten days, the idol becomes the centre of friendship, service and gratitude. Children learn the stories, elders share blessings and every helping hand becomes part of the prayer.

At visarjan, we carry Ganesha with music and hopeful hearts. The farewell reminds us that forms may return to the earth, but devotion stays with us. We promise to meet every new beginning with wisdom, courage and kindness, saying together: Ganapati Bappa Morya.
